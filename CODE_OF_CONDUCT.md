# Code of Conduct

Orrery Labs is a place to learn. We expect everyone taking part to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to a subject;
- critique work, not people;
- give credit for others' ideas and contributions;
- keep other people's personal information private.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are not allowed. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and block participants, who break these expectations.

To report a problem, open a confidential report through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and mark it "Conduct". It goes only to the maintainers.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
