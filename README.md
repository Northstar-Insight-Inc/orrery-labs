# Orrery Labs

**Learn it. Build it. Put it to work.**

Orrery Labs is the free, open-source lab collection from [Orrery](https://orrery.online/), a Northstar Insight, Inc. initiative. Each lab teaches a real, working skill, the kind usually locked behind paid courses and certificates, with steps you can follow on your own machine and results a second person can check.

There is no account to create and nothing to pay. We collect no usage data.

## What is here

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`labs/`](labs/README.md) | Hands-on learning labs. Each has outcomes, steps, a way to check your work, and its known limits. |
| [`builds/`](builds/README.md) | One small open-source tool each quarter, built to take a repeated manual job off a team's plate. |
| [`tools/`](tools/check_labs.py) | The structure check that runs on every change. |

## The standard every lab meets

- **It produces a result you can check.** A fresh reader follows the steps and gets the outcome described in "Check your work".
- **It shows what fails, as well as what passes.** Each lab includes at least one example that should fail and explains why.
- **It runs on free, local tools.** No paid subscription or AI account is required to finish a lab.
- **It uses synthetic data only.** No real person's records appear in this repository.
- **It is honest about its limits.** Every lab says what it does not cover.
